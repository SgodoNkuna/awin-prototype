## Joining A-Win

1. Go to **Membership** (`/membership`) and submit an application.
2. You'll get a confirmation email once your application is received.
3. Once approved, you can log in at **Member Login** (`/auth`) and access your **Member Portal** (`/portal`) — your membership status, tier, and available documents.
4. If your account requires a password change on first login, you'll be prompted automatically — this is a one-time security step.

## Letter of Authority & Risk Profile (LOA/RPA)

If you're asked to complete a Letter of Authority and/or Risk Profile Analysis, this is a **ThuthukaSA** form (FSP No. 47992) — A-Win's appointed Financial Services Provider — not an A-Win form. Two versions exist:

- **`/loa`** — Letter of Authority only. A short form (about a minute), for people who just need the authorisation paperwork.
- **`/loa-rpa`** — Letter of Authority **and** full Risk Profile Analysis. Takes about 5 minutes and includes questions about your investment goals, income, and risk appetite, used to guide ThuthukaSA's advice.

Both forms:
- Can be signed by typing your name or drawing your signature.
- Require you to fill in every section before you can submit — if something's missing, the page will tell you exactly what and highlight it.
- Are submitted directly and confidentially to ThuthukaSA, in line with FAIS and POPIA — not shared with A-Win beyond the fact that you submitted one.
- Send you a confirmation email once submitted.
- Have a "Download the blank template" link if you want to see the unsigned form first.

## Getting help

Use the **Contact** page (`/contact`) for general A-Win questions. LOA/RPA questions should go to ThuthukaSA directly, since they're the Financial Services Provider handling that data.
